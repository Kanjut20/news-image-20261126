## The six stories (all events dated Thu 26 Nov 2026)

1. Taiwan — Government said Thu 26 Nov it is confident of keeping its
   existing 15% US tariff rate; Executive Yuan Vice Premier Cheng Li-chiun
   held a press conference on the morning of 26 Nov explaining the Section
   301 proposal (15%, lowest tier with Japan/S Korea/EU/Switzerland, 2,232
   items exempted). Sources: Reuters Taipei Nov 26 via SRN News;
   ABMedia.
2. Singapore — Ministry of Energy, Trade and Industry said Thu 26 Nov it
   "disagrees with the USTR's findings and we are very disappointed with
   the proposed actions"; a 20% tariff was proposed on Singapore's exports.
   Framed as the Thu 26 Nov statement, not the Wed 25 Nov USTR proposal.
   Sources: CNA / Red Hot Singapore, Nov 26; Morningstar/Dow Jones Nov 25
   23:23 ET (04:23 GMT Nov 26).
3. Europe — EU leaders met in Brussels Thu 26 Nov for a two-day summit to
   bridge divisions over a proposed €45 billion loan for Ukraine in 2027
   and the bloc's 2028–2034 budget. Sources: Reuters Brussels Nov 26; AP
   Brussels Nov 26.
4. UK — London Underground RMT drivers struck from noon Thu 26 Nov; no
   service on the Circle or Piccadilly lines all day, Waterloo & City
   closed after midday, no Tube service anywhere after 21:00. Dispute over
   a voluntary four-day week. Sources: TfL; Glasgow Times / PA, Nov 26.
5. USA — A rapidly intensifying nor'easter battered the US East Coast on
   Thanksgiving Day Thu 26 Nov: heavy rain, gusts 60–70 mph, coastal
   flooding at Thu morning high tides; NJ/NY states of emergency,
   thousands of flights delayed. Framed as the storm hitting Thu, NOT the
   Macy's parade postponement (announced Wed 25 Nov). Sources: The
   Traveler, Nov 26; NWS warnings Thu 07:00–Fri 06:00.
6. Indonesia — 13 miners found dead Thu 26 Nov inside a flooded illegal
   gold mine in Pongkor, Bogor, West Java; 13 others still missing and the
   search resumed Thu. Framed as the Thu recovery/confirmation, not the
   Mon flooding that trapped them. Sources: Reuters Jakarta Nov 26;
   China Daily Asia Nov 26.

Footer: Thanksgiving Day in the US (Thu 26 Nov); EU summit runs to Fri;
Spain holds snap elections Sun 29 Nov.
